# Direct Tigris generation contracts v1

Authority: `docs/plans/CMS2JC2_DIRECT_TIGRIS_GENERATION_PLAN.md`.
New `CMS2JC2_RESPONSE_TG_{PROTOCOL,IMPORT,STUDY,STAGE,GATE,RUN,REPORT}/v1` reuse the
existing immutable DEV submission journals/output receipts and GEN bundle and
membership. Existing GEN/PORT artifacts retain their meaning and validators.

Authenticate the completed GEN gate and fitted lineage; bind the reference
hash, exact source-compatibility evidence, inventory/profile/data root, review,
membership and bundle. Pin a new Tigris environment without modifying the donor.
Scientific source must be unchanged outside the pre-existing execution-only
allowlist. Claims precede TRAIN offline-only ROOT reads. No final-test access.
The TG_IMPORT is created only after full historical authentication; subsequent
validation checks its pinned donor stage/study, gate receipt/bytes and bundle,
not a new historical fit or repeated reconstruction of its selection.

64-jet cross-site tolerance: p4/tracking atol=1e-12, rtol=1e-10, everything else
exact. Gate serial/trace/process parity and full 10k screen/repeat parity within
Tigris are bitwise; 64-row physical/identity prefix digests also bind full runs
to the gate. Key digests are compared across all complete screen runs.
Cross-site coverage must be reported as 64, not 10k. No PORT_PACKET is needed.

Source/parents/output byte hashes, CPU-only scheduler evidence, measured resource
admission, root cap and free-space checks remain mandatory. Separately reviewed
gate and screen, no automatic submission/advancement, no mutation/deletion of
old studies. Results remain engineering evidence, not response qualification.
