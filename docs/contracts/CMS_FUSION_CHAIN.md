and linear-rejection recovery are separate from checkpoint selection. Every
registered result is retained, even when worse. Final test remains sealed.

dry by default. Only `AUTHORIZE CMS FUSION CHAIN 500K EXACT SPEC` authorizes live
submission; compatible genuine dense acceptance may be imported honestly.

campaign. Publication is immutable, hash chained, receipted and task locked.
