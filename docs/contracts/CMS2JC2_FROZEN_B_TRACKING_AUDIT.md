# Frozen B tracking audit v1

Authority: [implementation plan](../plans/CMS2JC2_FROZEN_B_TRACKING_AUDIT_PLAN.md).
Additive CMS2JC2_RESPONSE v1 kinds: DEV_B_TRACKING, DEV_B_REUSE,
DEV_B_TRACKING_ACCEPTANCE, DEV_B_TRACKING_SHARD, DEV_B_TRACKING_REPORT.
All are content hashed, parent bound, final_test_accessed=false. Original
fitted-response and development contracts are not changed.

Revalidate the exact B_L donor response, fit, four shards, report, receipts,
canonical memberships, ranges, physics source, environment and conventions.
No fit, rematching, JC2 access, observed-target generation, production qualification
or automatic winner. Fresh disjoint study; scheduler writes require reviewed
plan authorization and resource admission. Existing jobs/artifacts stay unchanged.

The four frozen variants FULL/TRACK_CENTRAL/DZ_CENTRAL/DZERR_CENTRAL change
only registered tracking residual coordinates. Output keys, PID/charge/validity,
four-vectors and unaffected tracking coordinates must be identical. FULL checks
exact production replay per jet, then exact historic populations/bins/flags
with 1e-10 tolerances for floating aggregate moments only.

Counters distinguish jet-replicas, emissions, applicable tracking-coordinate
exposures and invalid placeholders. Error responses and central/residual moments
are recorded in log(mm), not misleading physical-error units. Missing residuals
and identity fallbacks remain explicit. Selected cells are observed occupancy,
not model inventory or physical match truth. Only bounded aggregates and plots
are durable; conditional closure and bad outcomes cannot suppress rows or fail
jobs. Invalid inputs, corrupt lineage, nonfinite required values or failed
numerical replay fail closed. All reported replica summaries are descriptive.
