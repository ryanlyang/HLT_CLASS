Scientific authority: [active plan](../plans/LUKA_RECONSTRUCTION_REPEAT_HYDRA_PLAN.md).
All new artifacts use `LUKA_RECONSTRUCTION_REPEAT_<KIND>/v1`, content hashes,
explicit parents and inherited exploratory-development flags. Earlier diagnostic,
curriculum and reconstruction artifacts retain their original contracts.

`RECIPE` fixes six recipes, three fresh seeds, order, original scientific configs,
schedule semantics, global batch128, 30 epochs, FP32, and reporting windows.
`SPEC` binds the clean pushed implementation, immutable original58d0c0ed source,
completed1094dea4 benchmark and outputs, original environment, HLT populations,
TRAIN U100 bank, complete OFFLINE inventory and resource envelope. Registration
requires a separate new root and at least13 personally available GiB. Nothing
is copied or modified in the old campaigns.

`QUALIFICATION` and `PARITY_STEP` bind 24 common-start comparisons: six recipes
at positions3,3.01,4.5,9, with full pre-update model/optimizer reference snapshots.
Dropout-disabled numerical parity uses loss absolute1e-3, gradients/parameters/
AdamW moments relative L2 5e-4, and buffers absolute1e-4. Named buffers and integer
counters, per-rank synchronization, complete coverage and finite diagnostics
are authenticated. No independent-trajectory tolerance or accuracy gate.

`RESOURCES`, `ACCEPTANCE`, `REPLAY`, `STARTUP` and `TASK` bind actual four-card
allocation, full support, full validation, measurements, all18 production
miniatures and deployment reloads. A warm measurement at position20 activates
every full coefficient, including10. The fit request is measured with2x safety

`INITIALIZATION` binds native/predictor/decoder state hashes, first-epoch full
permutation hash, seed identities and rank dropout seeds. Same-seed native,
predictor, data order and dropout starts must match; all five reconstruction
decoders in that group must also match. Setup requires all18 identities. Reports
compare available identities and mark incomplete groups explicitly.

`EPOCH`, `TRAINING_REPORT`, `DEPLOYMENT` and `FIT` bind full-validation metrics,
the original checkpoint selection key, per-epoch losses/gradient diagnostics,
native-only selected checkpoint, full training checkpoint and identity-aligned
selected probabilities. Science has exactly30 epochs; technical miniatures have
two synthetic positions7/20. No target, predictor or decoder enters deployment.
Missing/corrupt/changed epoch files, nonfinite required quantities or mismatched
native export fail closed. Finite poor recovery never fails execution.

`PLAN`, `SUBMIT_CLAIM`, `RUN_CLAIM` and exact-DAG journal/ledger contracts bind
commands and exact IDs. Bootstrap queues setup_pascal and its afterok CPU
release_pascal. Release authenticates successful setup and submits18 independent

Dry review covers the full resource envelope before bootstrap. Only measured
walltimes may change afterward. Completed exact submissions are idempotent;
partial/ambiguous journals stop for inspection. No automatic retry or fallback.

`RESULTS` authenticates completed receipts, histories and same-population
probabilities; `PROGRESS` reads authenticated atomic epoch files without inference.
Both use historical M0HLT/OFFLINE recovery anchors. Summaries disclose completed
seed and matched-pair denominators. A three-seed conclusion requires allthree;
missing controls never become zero delta. Epoch21-30 mean is unavailable until
the whole window exists; partial observed means are separately labelled.
Counts/streaks at52%, last recovery and selected recovery describe individual
checkpoints, not ensembles. No independent-test claim or validation-based seed
selection is permitted.
