## REFERENCE_ARCHIVE/v1: comparison-only legacy import

New reference imports contain a separate `REFERENCE_ARCHIVE` proof with
`reuse_scope=archival_comparison_only`, `metrics_recomputed=true`, and false
`legacy_preflight_reusable`, `model_or_target_reuse_authorized`, and
`checkpoint_and_target_payloads_revalidated`. Parents bind the new shared anchor,
original SPEC content hash and original RESULTS content hash. The proof records
original source/gate/preflight/aggregate/report/receipt hashes, the aggregate's
declared dependency map, SHA256/size refs for files actually verified, and the
explicit list of unverified legacy payload categories.

The original runtime verifies metadata and recomputes all six saved comparisons
from authenticated predictions. Every selected training report, full validation
population, original50k join, aggregate row and contrast must agree. Six training
receipts must match the aggregate's dependency hashes and registered immediate
teacher-export identities. Export payloads and their internal lineage are NOT
revalidated; this proof is never a substitute for a reusable bank/checkpoint
receipt. An absent/corrupt required report, prediction, cost record, aggregate or
registration still fails closed. No fallback to printed tables or presence checks.

The import and its archive proof are embedded in the new hashed gate; copied
predictions are separately bound in REFERENCES. New registration does not
reinterpret old preflight acceptance or relax either old or new science gates.
After snapshotting, report readers validate the copied evidence without reopening
the archival campaign. This adds reporting semantics only: recipe, losses,
retirement rules, selection, partitions and fresh-preflight requirements stay fixed.
